The look

Big yellow captions, numbers that hit

will bring you $950–$1,100
a week
  • Captions: large, bold, uppercase, two to three words at a time, in yellow with a heavy outline so they read on any background.
  • Numbers: every spoken figure slams in oversized with a zoom hit and a sound accent.
  • Framing: chest-up on Esad, with punch-ins on key lines. No jump cuts between near-identical shots.
  • Cutaways: the facade, kitchen, bathroom and floor plan images appear as he mentions them, with slow movement.
  • Sound: voice cleaned and levelled, pauses and filler removed, music under the voice, every video at the same loudness (-14 LUFS).
  • End card: the call to action he says on camera ("Click the link below"). No company name or logo is used until you confirm the brand.
Quality control

Each video is reviewed before the next one starts

Voice

Every line is Esad's own take, confirmed by mouth movement. No off-camera voice, no swearing, no banter.

Words

The finished audio is re-transcribed. Captions and numbers must match what he says exactly.

Cuts

The frames before and after every cut are checked for jumps, flashes and stray frames.

Sound

Checked for clicks, dead air, volume jumps and loudness.

Screen

No text off-screen, over his face, or running onto the end card.

A video that fails any check is fixed and reviewed again. It is only marked done when all five pass.

Delivery

One folder, clearly named

  • Vertical 9:16, 1080 × 1920, ready for Reels, TikTok, Shorts and Meta ads.
  • Two subfolders: Short Form and Long Form.
  • Files named by number and title, for example DualLiving_S1_VictoriasNewestInvestment.mp4.
To confirm with Omer and Esad

Three questions that don't block the edit

  • Brand name and logo. In the footage someone says not to use the company name, so the ads carry none. Send a logo if you want it on the end card.
  • Contact detail. The ads say "click the link below". Is there a phone number or website to show as well?
  • Rent and price figures. The ads state $750,000 and $950–$1,100 a week as he says them. Do you want a small "estimated" or "approximate" line on screen with those figures?
