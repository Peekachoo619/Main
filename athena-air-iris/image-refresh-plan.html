How it works

From your photos to finished images

  1. Reference pack

    I cut out your photos and record Iris's exact proportions, colours and details. Every image is checked against this pack.

  2. Generate variants

    I recreate each scene across the three models, using your product photos as references.

  3. Product lock

    I correct the best results by hand so the dryer matches the real Iris exactly.

  4. You choose

    You get 3–4 variants per image. Pick one, ask for changes, or mix elements from several.

  5. Finish and deliver

    Final images in 4K, plus web-ready versions cropped for desktop and mobile.

  • Soft daylight near a window, with no direct sun
  • Plain background, white or light grey
  • Step back about 1 m and zoom to 2×
  • Each colourway you'd like shown on the site
  • Send originals through Google Drive or email
  • No wide-angle or 0.5× lens, and no filters

Why step back and zoom? Phone cameras up close stretch whatever is nearest the lens, so the dryer head looks bigger than it is. That's the same proportion problem we're fixing, so the reference photos need to be distortion-free.
